About Us

Telling the greatest story in a way everyone can see

Gospel Animation is a Christian animation ministry from Nigeria. We create animated stories, devotional content and music that bring the message of the Bible to life for people of every age, in language and pictures that are simple to understand and beautiful to watch. Our work begins with Scripture and ends with a single hope: that someone who watches will feel closer to God.

We believe that a story well told can open a heart that a sermon alone might not reach. Animation lets us show the courage of David, the patience of Noah and the compassion of Jesus in a way children can follow and adults can still be moved by. Every frame we make is shaped by prayer, careful study of the Word and a commitment to honour the text we are retelling.

Gospel Animation is also the team behind Filtbo, a free Bible app that lets people read, search, listen to and study the Bible on their phones. Filtbo grew out of the same desire that drives our animations: to make Scripture easy to reach and a joy to return to every day, wherever a person lives and whatever device they own.

We are a young and growing ministry, and we are grateful for every person who watches, shares and prays with us. We publish across YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Spotify and a WhatsApp channel, because we want to meet people where they already spend their time and offer them something that lifts the heart instead of draining it.

“Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.”Psalm 119:105
Our Vision

A world that meets the Word through every screen

Our vision is to become a trusted home for faith-filled animation across Africa and around the world. We see a generation that grows up with Bible stories that look as exciting as the cartoons and films it already loves, and with Christian content that is made with the same skill, care and imagination as anything else on its screen. We want parents to feel safe handing a phone to their child, and we want teenagers to find something that speaks to them without talking down to them.

We also see a future where nobody is too far, too busy or too poor to hear the Gospel. That is why we build things that are free to watch, easy to share and light enough to work on ordinary phones and ordinary data plans. We want to use creativity and technology as tools of service, so that a person in a small town can open an app, hear a Bible story, and know that God sees them.

Finally, our vision is about quality and character. We want Gospel Animation to be known for excellence in craft and honesty in message: work that is beautiful, faithful to Scripture, and respectful of every viewer. We do not chase noise. We want to build slowly, pray often, and leave behind work that still encourages people many years from now.

What We Offer

Faith, told with imagination

Everything we make is built around one purpose: to help people encounter the Word of God in a fresh and memorable way. Some of our work is for quiet moments, such as a short devotional or a song for prayer. Some is for families to watch together, and some is a tool you keep on your phone and open every morning.

Whatever the format, we aim for the same standard of beauty, clarity and reverence. Our content is free to enjoy, and we welcome people of every denomination and background who love the Bible. Here is a look at what we offer today and what we are building toward.

What We Stand For

The anchor beneath everything we make

Our logo is an anchor, and it is chosen on purpose. Scripture calls hope “an anchor for the soul, firm and secure”, and we want our work to be a steady place for people to stand in a noisy and uncertain world. These values guide every project we begin and every decision we make as a team.

I

Faithful to Scripture

We stay close to the Bible and tell its stories with honesty and reverence.

II

Excellence

We give our best craft to every frame, because the message deserves it.

III

Family-Friendly

Our content is safe, respectful and welcoming for every age.

Our App

Filtbo, the Bible in your pocket

Filtbo is our free Bible app, built so that reading the Word can become a daily habit instead of a good intention. It brings the Bible, a study companion and gentle daily reminders together in one simple app that you can install straight from your browser.

  • Bible reading in several translations
  • Audio Bible you can listen to
  • Bible search by verse or topic
  • Reading plans with daily reminders
  • Verse images to share
  • Filtbo AI to help you study
